Our Story

School History

AR-RISALAH ACADEMY Nursery & Basic School was founded with a singular vision: to provide the children of Saki and its environs with an education that honours both their Islamic identity and their need for world-class academic knowledge.

The name "Ar-Risalah" (الرسالة) means "The Message" — reflecting our commitment to delivering the message of knowledge, moral excellence, and Islamic values to every child in our care.

Located at Alh. Zakariyah Salaam Street, behind Suurulere Kaomi Central Mosque, Saki, we serve families from Crèche through Basic 6 with qualified teachers, modern facilities, and affordable fees.

Founded

School Established

AR-Risalah Academy Nursery & Basic School opened its doors in Saki, Oyo State, under the proprietorship of Mr. Ashiru Ismail Adekunle.

Early Years

Nursery Programme Launched

Launched full Nursery 1–3 programme with Islamic curriculum integration, Qur'an memorisation and western education.

Growth

Basic School Expanded

Expanded to full Basic 1–6, adding Computer Studies, Arabic Language, and comprehensive Islamic Studies to the curriculum.

2025/2026

Digital ERP System

Launched fully computerised result management, online portals for parents, teachers, and students — a new era of excellence.

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To provide quality education that integrates Islamic values, moral character development, and western academic excellence — producing well-rounded, God-fearing, and academically sound young Muslims who will contribute positively to society.

Our Vision

To be the leading Islamic nursery and basic school in Oyo State — recognised for academic excellence, moral uprightness, spiritual development, and producing graduates who excel in both this world and the hereafter.
